About me

I design for the experience, not just the outcome.

I’m a Senior UX / Product Designer with 5+ years of experience designing digital products, from defining problems to delivering solutions.

Much of my work has been in complex B2B SaaS environments, where workflows, business rules, and different user needs intersect. I enjoy making sense of that complexity and turning it into experiences that feel clear, coherent, and easier to navigate.

What I believe

I believe good design should leave people feeling they did their work well, not just that they got through it.

The experience should support them, not wear them down.

How I think and work

I start by asking why.

I want to understand the problem beneath the request, what people are trying to achieve, and what is getting in their way.

Beyond an individual screen.

From research and workflow mapping to interaction design, prototyping, and validation, I work methodically to find solutions that make sense beyond an individual screen. I look at how the pieces connect, how decisions affect the wider system, and how the experience can work better as a whole.

Better together.

I enjoy working closely with product and engineering teams, bringing different perspectives together and helping turn complex requirements into practical solutions. I try to be the kind of teammate who makes the process more collaborative and the problem easier to solve.

The work I do

My experience spans enterprise procurement, travel management, and online video platforms. I’ve worked across end-to-end product design, mobile experiences, and design systems, helping products become more consistent, usable, and scalable.
